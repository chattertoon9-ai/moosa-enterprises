Fly Ash: Meaning, Uses in Construction & Environmental Benefits

Meaning

Fly ash is the fine grey powder left over after coal is burned in a power plant. It is a pozzolanic material — it reacts with lime in concrete — which makes it work well as a partial replacement for cement.

Uses in Construction

  • Concrete and mortar
  • Concrete blocks and pavers
  • Road bases and embankments
  • Grouting and filling work

Environmental Benefits

  • Using less cement lowers the CO₂ emissions of concrete
  • Reuses a power-plant byproduct instead of sending it to landfill
  • Improves the durability and workability of concrete
