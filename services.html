What We Do

Our Services

Eight service areas covering mechanical and civil works, road construction, materials, scrap, waste and industrial supplies.

Mechanical Engineering, Piping & Structure Maintenance

Fabrication and installation of piping, structures and supports; erection for chemical plants and oil & gas fields. Work on compressors, turbines, pumping stations, boilers, heat exchangers, pressure vessels and storage tanks — plus plant maintenance, heat exchanger cleaning, painting, corrosion protection and sand & grid blasting.

Civil Construction

Construction, structure and maintenance work including painting, PVC products and FRP coating. Planning for houses, plazas, bridges and towers; RCC foundations and excavations, earth works, tiling and finishing.

Road Construction

Roadway design and engineering, and roadway construction including earthworks, grading and paving. Pavement construction and rehabilitation, road maintenance and upgrades.

Solid Concrete Blocks

Custom block production in the sizes and strengths your project requires, manufactured under strict quality control. Bulk supply for large-scale projects with delivery to site, for retaining walls and landscaping — with fire resistance, durability and technical consultation.

Wood Scrap & Iron Scrap

Wood and iron scrap dealing with local and international stock, serving industrial plants, petrochemical, energy, infrastructure and general construction clients.

Garbage Collection

Waste and garbage collection for clients across the region, carried out by specialist teams.

General Items Supply

Electrical, non-electrical, mechanical and non-mechanical items, and all kinds of tools used at power plants — procured cost-efficiently, directly from the source.

Fly-ash, Gypsum & Bolter

Fly-ash, gypsum and bolter dealing for industrial plants, process and petrochemical industries, energy and infrastructure.
